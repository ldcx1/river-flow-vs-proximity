  NOTE: BH here is two-sided (p from |point|/SE), so a Family-B 'survivor'
  can be an effect significant against the graph. Attribution vs
  random, Propagation turbidity, and Attribution vs euclidean are graph losses,
  not wins. The positive graph survivors are the ones named below.

Robust conclusion:
  The graph-structure effects (co-fluctuation, break, tracing, attribution
  direction, conditional, basins) survive BH under both families and a single
  uniform p-method. At the full ~65-comparison universe the co-fluctuation,
  conditional, attribution-direction and basin-scaling effects still survive; the
  break effect survives only at m<=65 and drops out for any larger family (its p
  sits on the FDR boundary), so we treat it as boundary-sensitive and do not
  headline it. The tracing gain and the fusion gain do not survive (tracing is
  partly definitional, fusion withdrawn). Recovery/denoise are reported as
  synthetic-injection stability, not population effects.
